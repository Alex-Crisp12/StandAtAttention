The game area is a size of 32 by 18, with 0,0 being the center.
Up and Right are both positive (like in desmos, yippee)

The point input action is used for the 3 different input types.

T toggles which of the three inputs is being used (mouse, eye and controller gyro).


Player is a rigidbody circle that can jump (pretty simple)

Making permanent assets layer order 0 or less, things that are off by default 1 and things that are on by default 1.
